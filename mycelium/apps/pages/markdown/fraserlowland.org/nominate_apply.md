## Why Nominations Matter

Some of the most important changemakers:

- Don't think of themselves as "leaders", and often wrestle with imposter syndrome
- Sit at intersections that don't fit neat categories
- Have acclimatized to there being minimal support for themselves and their work
- Are trusted locally, even if not visible publicly

Your nomination helps us reach people who might otherwise be missed.

This is not about status, polish or organizational backing.

**It's about impact, potential, integrity, and care.**

---

## Who We're Looking For

We're seeking emergent leaders and changemakers who are:

### Rooted in Place

- Living, working, and deeply connected to the Fraser Lowland
- Engaged with real communities, ecosystems, or systems here

### Actively Engaged

- Already doing meaningful work (paid or unpaid)
- Taking responsibility, initiating action, or holding important roles
- Not just theorizing—participating

### Oriented Toward Care & Collaboration

- Values relationship, reciprocity, and long-term thinking
- Open to learning with others across differences
- Willing to share, reflect, and be in dialogue

### Working Within (or Across) One or More Domains

Participants may be working in areas such as:

- Ecological restoration or land stewardship
- Food systems, housing, or local economies
- Health, care, and community wellbeing
- Arts, culture, and storytelling
- Governance, facilitation, or systems change
- Education, learning, or youth work
- Technology in service of community
- Indigenous, diasporic, or marginalized community leadership

**Formal credentials are not required.**
