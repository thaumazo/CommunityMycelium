## Nominate/ Someone

Thank you for helping us find people doing important work in the Fraser Lowland.

### Who We're Looking For

We're seeking people already making their communities stronger - often without much recognition, funding, or formal support. They're:

**Already taking action** - not just thinking about change, but doing something, showing up consistently, or holding space for others

**Rooted in this place** - living and working in the Fraser Lowland, connected to real communities here

**Oriented toward care** - valuing relationships and long-term thinking, willing to learn with others

#### Examples of People You Might Nominate

**Community food work** Someone running a free meal program, feeding 50 people weekly and coordinating volunteers - all from their own kitchen with no budget.

**Neighborhood organizing** A young person organizing their apartment building to share resources, watch each other's kids, and create a community garden in an unused lot.

**Cultural knowledge keeping** An elder teaching traditional plant knowledge to a small group, helping people reconnect with the land and cultural practices at risk of being lost.

**Emergency preparedness** Someone coordinating disaster readiness in their neighborhood, making sure everyone has what they need and knows their neighbors.

**Holding difficult conversations** An artist creating spaces for conversations about racism, housing, or climate - holding complexity with care.

**Building repair networks** A person creating systems for fixing things instead of throwing them away, teaching skills and building community.

**Youth mentorship & skill-sharing** Someone teaching young people trades, life skills, or offering consistent support and guidance.

**Land & ecosystem care** A person restoring habitat, stewarding watersheds, or protecting green spaces.

**Housing & economic solutions** Someone working on co-housing, tool libraries, time banks, or other ways of sharing resources.

This could be paid work, volunteer work, or something in between. No formal credentials needed.

### What Happens After You Nominate

Your nomination opens a door:

1. We reach out to invite them to join the Localism community
2. They choose their level of participation - from online gatherings to deeper involvement
3. Up to 32 people are invited into facilitated sessions in February
4. 6-8 projects receive $2,500 to support a one-month project in March

Everyone benefits from connection, learning, and community support. Not everyone receives funding, but everyone is part of building something meaningful together.

## Why Your Nomination Matters

Some of the most important changemakers in our region:

- Don't think of themselves as "leaders" and often wrestle with imposter syndrome
- Sit at intersections that don't fit neat categories  
- Have gotten used to minimal support for themselves and their work
- Are trusted locally, even if not visible publicly

Your nomination helps us reach people who might otherwise be missed. This is not about status, polish, or organizational backing. It's about impact, potential, integrity, and care.
