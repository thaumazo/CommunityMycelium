# 👥 People of the Fraser Lowland

*Those who care for this place.*

---

## **🌿 Why This Page Exists**

Regenerating a bioregion isn’t a project — it’s a web of relationships.
This page highlights the people who are actively tending to the Fraser Lowland: land stewards, community builders, artists, technologists, Elders, youth, disruptors, healers, organizers, and quiet doers.

Each person here is connected by care for this place and a desire to participate in its renewal.

---

## **🧭 What You’ll Find Below**

An interactive directory where you can:

* **Browse profiles** of people working across ecology, culture, economy, education, policy, technology, and community care.
* **See roles and offerings**, such as:

  * 🌱 Land & Water Steward
  * 🎨 Cultural Worker / Storyteller
  * 🛠 Builder / Maker / Innovator
  * 🤝 Facilitator / Organizer / Connector
  * 🌀 System Thinker / Researcher / Policy Shaper
* **Explore gifts and needs** — what people can offer and where they are looking for collaboration or support.
* **Connect with others** to learn, co-create, mentor, or simply be in relationship.

---

## **💫 Guiding Spirit**

This space is built on the belief that:

* Everyone has a role.
* Leadership is shared and emergent.
* Wisdom lives in diverse experiences, cultures, and ways of knowing.
* Strong relationships are the foundation of resilient systems.

You don’t need a title to belong here — just curiosity, care, and a willingness to contribute.

---

## **📌 How to Join**

If you live in or are connected to the Fraser Lowland and want to be part of this regenerative weave:

1. **Create or update your profile**
   Share your story, roles, skills, passions, and what you care about in this place.

2. **List your offerings and needs**
   What can you give to the bioregion? What support would help you do your work?

3. **Connect with others**
   Reach out for collaboration, mentorship, volunteering, or shared learning.
