# 🛠️ Projects of the Fraser Lowland

*Real work. Real places. Regenerating the bioregion together.*

---

## **🌿 What Counts as a Project?**

A project is any effort — large or small — that helps repair, restore, or reimagine life in the Fraser Lowland.

This includes:

* **Hands-on ecological restoration** — stream rehabilitation, wetland revival, native plant propagation, salmon habitat protection.
* **Social resilience & mutual aid** — community kitchens, housing initiatives, disaster response networks, harm reduction projects.
* **Cultural & storytelling work** — archives, art installations, documentary projects, language revitalization, place-based education.
* **Local economy & food systems** — community farms, co-ops, skillshares, land trusts, regenerative business models.
* **Technology & systems tools** — open-source mapping, data sovereignty platforms, crisis coordination tools, bioregional governance prototypes.
* **Youth-led and grassroots experiments** — pop-up collaborations, school projects, river cleanups, neighbourhood labs.

If it helps the land, people, or communities of this bioregion thrive — it belongs here.

## **🔧 Project Lifecycle (Metachrysalis Framework)**

| Stage                   | Description                                                            |
| ----------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 🌱 **Seed**             | An idea, question, or proposal — seeking partners, land, or resources. |
| 🌿 **Sprout**           | Early pilots, prototypes, experiments — learning by doing.             |
| 🌾 **Growing**          | Active project with people, resources, and momentum.                   |
| 🌳 **Established**      | Stable, rooted, and contributing lasting value to the bioregion.       |
| 🍄 **Myceliating**      | Sharing learnings, replicating, or weaving with other initiatives.     |
| 🍂 **Dormant/Complete** | Paused, completed, archived — still valuable as story or learning.     |
