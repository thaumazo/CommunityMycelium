# 🦋 Metachrysalis  
*A real-world cooperative game for collective flourishing*

---

## Welcome
**Metachrysalis** is a living framework, community process, and real-world game that helps people and organizations collaborate toward regenerative futures.  
It bridges **play, purpose, and praxis** — transforming the *Metacrisis* into a *Metachrysalis*: a stage of collective metamorphosis where new systems can emerge.

Here, you can:
- Explore how the *Metacrisis* and *Metachrysalis* are unfolding in your **bioregion**.  
- Map your **skills, roles, and resources** as a changemaker.  
- Connect with **people, communities, and projects** around shared purpose.  
- Make **Flourishing Moves** — real-world actions that strengthen your place and your network.

---

## 🌍 The Vision
A world where every community becomes a regenerative ecosystem — ecologically vibrant, socially just, and resilient.  
Metachrysalis turns complexity into collaboration by treating the great work of our time as a **game we play together**.

---

## 🎲 How the Game Works
Each local or online session follows four phases:

1. **Gathering Players & Setting the Stage**  
   Orient around shared purpose, place, and potential.  

2. **Character & Party Creation**  
   Create “changemaker characters” that represent your gifts, passions, and capacities.  

3. **Exploring the Gameboard**  
   Use localized cards to explore how the *Metacrisis* and *Metachrysalis* show up in your bioregion.  

4. **Collective Quests & Flourishing Moves**  
   Design and enact real-world projects — from mutual aid to bioregional transformation.  

Past Moves become part of the living *Open-Source Playbook* for others to learn from and build upon.

---

## 🧩 Core Principles
- **Nestedness** — work is rooted in bioregions, defined by natural systems rather than borders.  
- **Play as Practice** — games foster imagination, experimentation, and trust.  
- **Symbiotic Roles** — leadership is distributed; everyone contributes from their strengths.  
- **Transparency & Trust** — open data and clear communication sustain collaboration.  
- **Regeneration > Extraction** — every action restores ecological, social, cultural, and economic vitality.

---

## 🕸️ The Infrastructure
Metachrysalis weaves together open tools and living culture:

- **Community Mycelium Platform** — mapping people, projects, and flows of capital.  
- **Skill Trees & Microcredentials** — growing mentorship networks and visible capacities.  
- **Permaculture Capital Framework** — tracking ecological, social, and cultural wealth.  
- **Story & Game Assets** — physical and digital decks, quests, and rituals for meaningful play.  
- **Open-Source Playbook** — a living library of regenerative practices co-authored by players.  
- **Community Space** *(in partnership with the Design School for Regenerating Earth)* — a shared Mighty Networks hub for gatherings, learning, and collaboration.

## 🕹️ Game Modes
Metachrysalis adapts to what’s needed in the moment:

| Mode | Focus |
|------|-------|
| **Relief** | Rapid coordination and mutual aid during emergencies. |
| **Resilience** | Strengthening communities between crises. |
| **Regeneration** | Long-term transformation of systems and culture. |

---

## 🌱 Origins
Metachrysalis grew from **Daniel Lindenberger’s “Mycelial Approaches to the Metacrisis” Fellowship**, synthesizing five years of work with emergent communities including:

- **CoronaWhy** — open ML research for COVID-19.  
- **Ukraine Now** — crowdsourced refugee and evacuation support.  
- **Western North Carolina Relief Networks** — citizen-led disaster coordination after Hurricane Helene.  

Pilots are now active in **Vancouver**, **Langley**, **Salt Spring Island**, **Milwaukee**, and **North Carolina**.
