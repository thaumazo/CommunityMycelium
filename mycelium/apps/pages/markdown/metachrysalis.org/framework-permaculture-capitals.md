# The 8 Permaculture Capitals
*Multiple forms of value, not only money.*

---

![Capitals image](../../../../static/img/misc/pexels-weavehall-collective-746895245-18649994.jpg)

## Capitals
| Image | Title | Text |
|---|---|---|
| <img src="../../../../static/img/capitals/01_financial.png" alt="Financial" width="240"> | Financial | Money, currencies, credit, investments, and tokens of exchange value. |
| <img src="../../../../static/img/capitals/02_material.png" alt="Material" width="240"> | Material | The tangible, human-made or harvested objects: buildings, tools, vehicles, infrastructure, renewable tech, local goods, fiber, fuel. |
| <img src="../../../../static/img/capitals/03_natural.png" alt="Natural" width="240"> | Natural | The living and non-living systems of the bioregion: forests, rivers, soil, air, minerals, biodiversity, climate, fungi, wetlands. |
| <img src="../../../../static/img/capitals/04_social.png" alt="Social" width="240"> | Social | Relationships, trust, networks, cooperation, shared norms, and mutual aid. |
| <img src="../../../../static/img/capitals/05_intellectual.png" alt="Intellectual" width="240"> | Intellectual | Knowledge, systems thinking, know-how, design methods, data, patterns, and models. |
| <img src="../../../../static/img/capitals/06_experiential.png" alt="Experiential" width="240"> | Experiential | Embodied practice, lessons learned, skillsets earned through time and doing, often tacit or locally specific. |
| <img src="../../../../static/img/capitals/07_cultural.png" alt="Cultural" width="240"> | Cultural | Art, language, shared symbols, heritage, traditions, rituals, values, and aesthetic ways of being. |
| <img src="../../../../static/img/capitals/08_spiritual.png" alt="Spiritual" width="240"> | Spiritual | Meaning-making, connection to the sacred, inner transformation, purpose, awe, and alignment with something greater. |
