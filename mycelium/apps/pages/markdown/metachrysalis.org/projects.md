# 🌻 Projects  
*Flourishing Moves in the game of collective regeneration.*

---

## 💫 What Are Projects?
In **Metachrysalis**, projects are the *real-world quests* where imagination meets action.  
They are the “Flourishing Moves” that players and communities take together to bring regeneration to life — in neighborhoods, ecosystems, and cultures around the world.

Each project is both a **learning journey** and a **contribution** to the greater metamorphosis we are enacting together.

---

## 🎯 Purpose
Projects turn the principles of Metachrysalis into tangible outcomes:
- Restoring **ecosystems** and regenerating land.  
- Strengthening **communities** and local economies.  
- Reviving **cultures** and creating new stories of belonging.  
- Building **infrastructure** for resilience and collaboration.  
- Exploring **governance and finance** that serve life, not extraction.  

No project is too small — even the smallest act of restoration, care, or connection is a move toward flourishing.

---

## 🪶 Flourishing Moves
Every completed project becomes part of the **living record of the game**, a story others can learn from and build upon.

When you document your Move, you’re invited to share:
- **Intent & Context** — what challenge or opportunity inspired it.  
- **Collaborators** — the people and communities involved.  
- **Resources & Capitals** — what forms of value were exchanged (natural, social, cultural, financial, etc.).  
- **Outcomes & Insights** — what was learned, changed, or regenerated.  
- **Next Steps** — how others can continue or replicate your work.

In this way, the Metachrysalis becomes a **playbook of regeneration**, authored collectively through lived experience.

---

## 🌍 Scales of Action
Projects can take many forms, across multiple scales:

| Scale | Example | Focus |
|--------|----------|--------|
| **Personal** | Composting system, garden, or creative ritual. | Cultivating regenerative habits. |
| **Local** | Neighborhood mutual aid, river cleanup, art festival. | Strengthening community ties. |
| **Bioregional** | Watershed restoration, circular economy network. | Coordinated systemic change. |
| **Inter-Bioregional** | Cross-region research, open-source toolbuilding, fellowship programs. | Sharing knowledge and scaling impact. |

Each level contributes to the whole, mirroring how mycelial networks grow — from small threads into vast living systems.

---

## 🕹️ Game Integration
Projects are the **action layer** of the Metachrysalis game.

They connect directly to:
- **People** — changemakers with defined roles, archetypes, and skills.  
- **Communities** — the networks and organizations that host them.  
- **Permaculture Capitals** — the multidimensional flows of value they generate.  
- **Skill Trees** — the learning pathways that evolve through doing.  

Over time, this creates a map of transformation — a record of how we, collectively, are turning crisis into chrysalis.

## 🔄 Modes of Play
Projects, like communities, move through three primary modes:

| Mode | Focus | Example |
|------|--------|----------|
| **Relief** | Rapid response to crisis. | Emergency coordination, food distribution, resource mapping. |
| **Resilience** | Building strength between crises. | Tool libraries, community hubs, skill-sharing programs. |
| **Regeneration** | Long-term transformation. | Rewilding, bioregional planning, cooperative education. |

Your project might begin in one mode and evolve into another as context and capacity grow.
