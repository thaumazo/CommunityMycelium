# 👥 People  
*Meet the changemakers shaping our collective metamorphosis.*

---

## 🌱 Who We Are
Every player in **Metachrysalis** is a **changemaker** — someone bringing care, creativity, and courage to the work of regeneration.  
This page gathers people from across bioregions who are experimenting, collaborating, and playing toward a thriving future.

You can think of it as the **character roster** for our shared game — a living map of allies, mentors, and collaborators.

---

## 🧙🏽‍♀️ Create Your Character
In Metachrysalis, your *character* represents how you show up in the game of transformation.

Each person creates a profile that reflects:
- **Gifts & Capacities** — what you love doing and what you bring.  
- **Current Quests** — projects, missions, or experiments you’re part of.  
- **Alliances** — communities, organizations, or bioregions you’re connected to.  
- **Skill Trees** — the abilities and experiences you’re growing.  
- **Permaculture Capitals** — where your energy flows (social, cultural, natural, financial, etc.).  

Your character is not a mask — it’s an evolving reflection of your **authentic agency** in the web of life.  
As you learn and collaborate, your story, skills, and connections level up.

## 🪶 Roles & Archetypes
Every player brings something unique to the table.  
Some of the emergent archetypes within Metachrysalis include:

| Archetype | Focus |
|------------|--------|
| **Initiator** | Sparks new ideas and gathers people. |
| **Synthesizer** | Connects patterns, people, and knowledge. |
| **Implementer** | Turns ideas into grounded action. |
| **Storyweaver** | Makes meaning visible through art, narrative, and media. |
| **Guardian** | Protects integrity, wellbeing, and relational trust. |
| **Nurturer** | Builds care systems and cultivates community. |
| **Bridgebuilder** | Links organizations, sectors, and worldviews. |
| **Visionkeeper** | Holds the long view, guiding purpose and coherence. |

These roles are not fixed — they shift as conditions change and as you find new forms of collaboration.

## 🕸️ How to Engage
1. **Explore** this page to meet people working on projects that inspire you.  
2. **Connect** with others who share your interests, bioregion, or archetype.  
3. **Collaborate** on collective quests and “Flourishing Moves.”  
4. **Contribute** your story, resources, or wisdom to the open-source Playbook.  

Together, we form a distributed party of changemakers — a *mycelial guild* playing for planetary regeneration.
